## 🔍 Problem Statement
Zomato users who experience a delivery delay receive **zero 
proactive communication** from the platform. This silence turns 
an operationally inevitable inconvenience into a trust-destroying 
product failure — driving 1-star reviews, support tickets, 
and user churn.

---

## 📊 Key Findings
- Analyzed **50,000+ Zomato Play Store reviews** (Jan 2024 – Jun 2025)
- **Delivery delay = #1 pain point** — present in 48% of all 
  negative reviews
- Post-delay average rating: **2.1 / 5.0** (vs 3.8 target)
- Overall negative sentiment: **41.4%** of all reviews
- Critical churn risk users: **9.7%** of user base

---

## 💡 Solution — Delay Shield
A tiered auto-compensation system that fires proactively 
when a delivery exceeds its ETA:

| Delay | Response | Compensation |
|---|---|---|
| 5–15 mins | Push notification only | None |
| 15–30 mins | Notification + modal | ₹30 Zomato Cash |
| 30–60 mins | Full Delay Shield | ₹50 Zomato Cash |
| 60+ mins | Full + bonus | ₹50 Cash + Free delivery |
